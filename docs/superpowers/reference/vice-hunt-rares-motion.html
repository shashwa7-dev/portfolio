How they move (click to shoot)

A rough motion preview, not the game: every creature loops here so you can see them all. In the game they are rare. Click any of them to see its hit. The cow and the tiger cost points; Bigfoot and the tiger can hurt you (hearts, top left), but only after you hit them.

WASTED
Flying cow: floats slowly, bobbing, wings flapping. Hit 1: garland pops on, −500. Hit 2: −500, she deflates like a balloon and zigzags off.
Flying tiger: fast pass, quick wingbeats, tail swishing, swooping. Protected, three hits: the first two make it flinch (1 / 3, 2 / 3); the third is −250 and its claw costs 1.5 ♥: it pounces down onto the sand, crouches, then leaps straight at the screen (your point of view), hissing, and claws the glass. The scratches are the last beat: it drops out of view and only the marks stay, then fade.
Walking shark: struts upright, legs stepping, fins swinging like arms, a bounce on each step. Hit: +600, it turns into a rocket: crouches, ignites, and blasts straight up into the sky on a shower of fireworks spraying out underneath.
Merman: rises out of the sea, waves, dives back, pops up somewhere else. Needs three quick hits: the first makes him duck and start slipping under, and you have about a second for the other two, or he is gone and it resets. Third hit: +600, he drops under and the sea shoots up a fountain of small fish that arc over and dive back in.
UFO: hovers and wobbles, lights chasing, beam now and then. Tough: 6 or 7 hits; each one sparks and jolts it, and it smokes and wobbles more as it takes damage. Last hit: +800, sparks and smoke, spirals into the sea; the alien bails out (the dome is empty from then on) and parachutes down, swaying, into the water.
Bigfoot: harmless until you hit him. First hit: ANGRY!, and he keeps walking while throwing his poop at the screen every 1.5 s until he is off it. Each splat blocks part of your view for a few seconds and costs ½ ♥, up to 1.5 ♥ in all from one Bigfoot (later throws still splat, but cost nothing). A boss: 20 hits, shown by a bar over his head. Twentieth hit: +1500, he squats, strains, PFFT!, and jets off diagonally, splatting the screen as he goes.
